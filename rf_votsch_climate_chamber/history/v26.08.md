# v26.08 — Real-Hardware Verification Hardening

Date: 2026-07-31

## Corrected

- Replaced one-shot setpoint readback verification with bounded polling.
- Added configurable setpoint verification timeout and polling interval.
- Made `Get Driver Configuration` output safely re-importable by ignoring its read-only runtime annotations during validation.
- Made explicit `Disconnect`, `Disconnect All`, and suite-listener cleanup obey the same effective `safe_shutdown_on_disconnect` policy.
- Prevented `Safe Shutdown` from writing model-dependent dryer or compressed-air channels unless those channels are explicitly configured.
- Updated hardware smoke and all-API suites to assert that close-only configuration was actually applied.

## Safety behavior

Real hardware now defaults to no auxiliary-output mapping. `Safe Shutdown` always attempts to stop the chamber, but records unconfigured auxiliary actions as `SKIP`. Operators may enable auxiliary control only after qualifying the chamber mapping and configuring:

- `settings.auxiliary_outputs.dryer_output_channel`
- `settings.auxiliary_outputs.compressed_air_output_channel`

The deterministic simulator continues to use reference channels 8 and 7.
